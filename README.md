**Teleport Gun**

How to use:
- Pick up the gun.
- Aim at blue boxes by moving the camera. If a blue box turns yellow, that means the bullet you shoot will hit it.
- Shoot using left click.
- Once you hit it, you swap positions with the blue box.
- If you are stood on top of a box and aim at it, the box will sometimes turn red. This means it's inactive and you can't hit it until you move.
